Coverage requires GCC or Clang plus `gcovr`; run via `cmake --preset linux-coverage` or `-DHSBA_COVERAGE=ON`, then `cmake --build --target coverage` — it fails when core/non-core line-coverage thresholds are not met. Version generation requires PowerShell (`pwsh` or `powershell`) available on PATH. Wiki export: `python scripts/repowiki_to_github_wiki.py [--lang en|zh|both] [--src .qoder/repowiki] [--out wiki-output]`. Static checks: `python static_check/cpp_analyzer.py <path>` and `python static_check/clang-format-batch.py [-j N] [--dry-run]`.
